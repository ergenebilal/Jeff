# 30-Day AI Agency Launch Roadmap

> Follow this day-by-day plan to go from zero to paying clients in 30 days.

---

## WEEK 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1-7)

### Day 1: Niche Selection
- [ ] Pick one industry (e.g., real estate, e-commerce, legal, healthcare)
- [ ] Identify the top 3 repetitive tasks in that industry
- [ ] Research what automations competitors are selling
- [ ] **Outcome:** Your niche + 3 service offerings defined

### Day 2: Service Packages
- [ ] Create 3 service tiers (use `pricing-tiers.md` template)
- [ ] Define what each tier includes and excludes
- [ ] Set pricing based on value (hours saved × $50/h × 3 months)
- [ ] **Outcome:** Pricing page ready

### Day 3: Portfolio — Build Reference Workflow #1
- [ ] Build first demo workflow (pick: content aggregator, lead capture, or chatbot)
- [ ] Record a 2-min Loom video showing it working
- [ ] Take screenshots of the workflow dashboard
- [ ] **Outcome:** First portfolio piece

### Day 4: Portfolio — Build Reference Workflow #2
- [ ] Build second demo workflow (complementary to #1)
- [ ] Record walkthrough
- [ ] Document the tech stack used
- [ ] **Outcome:** Two portfolio pieces

### Day 5: Online Presence
- [ ] Set up Gumroad store (already done for you)
- [ ] Create a simple landing page (squarespace or carrd)
- [ ] Connect your domain (e.g., yourname.ai)
- [ ] **Outcome:** Live website with portfolio

### Day 6: Lead Magnet
- [ ] Package one workflow as a free download
- [ ] Create a landing page: "Get [workflow name] free"
- [ ] Add email capture (Mailchimp / ConvertKit)
- [ ] **Outcome:** Lead magnet live, collecting emails

### Day 7: Rest & Review
- [ ] Review week 1 progress against checklist
- [ ] Prepare 5 outreach messages for week 2
- [ ] Schedule social media posts for the week ahead
- [ ] **Outcome:** Full readiness for outreach

---

## WEEK 2: OUTREACH (Days 8-14)

### Day 8-9: Prospect List
- [ ] Find 50 businesses in your niche on LinkedIn
- [ ] Identify decision-makers (CEO, COO, Head of Ops)
- [ ] Note their pain points from their LinkedIn content
- [ ] **Outcome:** 50-prospect list with notes

### Day 10: Cold Outreach — Batch 1 (15 prospects)
- [ ] Send personalized LinkedIn connection requests
- [ ] After connection, send value-first message (not pitch)
- [ ] **Tip:** "I noticed you [observation]. I built something that solved this for [similar company]. Want to see a 2-min demo?"
- [ ] **Outcome:** 5+ responses

### Day 11: Cold Outreach — Batch 2 (15 prospects)
- [ ] Repeat Day 10 pattern with next batch
- [ ] Follow up with any who responded but didn't book a call
- [ ] **Outcome:** 3+ discovery calls booked

### Day 12: Discovery Calls
- [ ] First discovery call (use onboarding checklist)
- [ ] Listen more than talk. Ask: "What's the most tedious part of your week?"
- [ ] Record the call (with permission)
- [ ] **Outcome:** 1 qualified lead

### Day 13: Discovery Calls
- [ ] Second and third discovery calls
- [ ] Send proposal to qualified lead from Day 12
- [ ] **Outcome:** 1 proposal sent

### Day 14: Rest & Review
- [ ] Review: how many calls booked? How many proposals sent?
- [ ] Refine outreach message based on responses
- [ ] **Outcome:** Outreach cadence optimized

---

## WEEK 3: FIRST SALE (Days 15-21)

### Day 15-16: Follow Up
- [ ] Follow up on all proposals sent
- [ ] Handle objections (price, timeline, trust)
- [ ] Offer a free 30-min "automation audit" to hesitant prospects
- [ ] **Outcome:** 1 signed agreement

### Day 17-19: Onboarding & Build
- [ ] Onboard first client (use `client-onboarding-checklist.md`)
- [ ] Start building their first workflow
- [ ] Send daily progress update
- [ ] **Outcome:** Client sees progress, builds trust

### Day 20-21: Deliver & Collect Testimonial
- [ ] Deploy first workflow
- [ ] Record the "before vs after" metrics
- [ ] Ask for testimonial + LinkedIn recommendation
- [ ] **Outcome:** First case study material

---

## WEEK 4: SCALE (Days 22-30)

### Day 22-24: Case Study
- [ ] Write up the first client case study
- [ ] Include: problem, solution, results (with numbers)
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn, website, and send to prospect list
- [ ] **Outcome:** Social proof asset

### Day 25-27: Batch Outreach — Round 2
- [ ] Use case study as opener: "I just helped [client] save 20h/week on [task]"
- [ ] Contact remaining prospects (20)
- [ ] Leverage referral from first client
- [ ] **Outcome:** 2+ new leads

### Day 28-29: Systemize
- [ ] Document your delivery process (use this roadmap!)
- [ ] Create standard operating procedures for recurring tasks
- [ ] Set up monthly retainer invoices
- [ ] **Outcome:** Scalable agency infrastructure

### Day 30: Review & Plan Month 2
- [ ] Revenue: $[X] from first client
- [ ] Pipeline: [X] active leads
- [ ] Goal for month 2: 3 clients / $5k MRR
- [ ] Adjust pricing if needed (you're probably undercharging)
- [ ] **Celebrate:** You launched an AI agency in 30 days 🎉

---

## ⚡️ SUCCESS ACCELERATORS

- **Niche down HARD:** "I help real estate agents automate their lead follow-up" beats "I do AI automation"
- **Price in value, not hours:** If you save a client 40h/month at $100/h, charge $2,000 — not $500
- **Over-deliver on first client:** They become your best salesperson
- **Automate your own agency:** Use n8n to track leads, send proposals, and follow up — eat your own dog food
