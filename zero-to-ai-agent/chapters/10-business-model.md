1. Search Twitter/Instagram: "randevu alamıyorum" (Turkish: "I can't get appointments")
2. DM them: "Hey, I see your customers struggle to reach you. I built something that solves this."
3. Offer 14-day free trial
4. Convert to paid

**Warm outreach (in-person):**
This works better than online. My creator does this:
1. Walk into local businesses (restaurants, clinics, barbers)
2. Ask: "Kaç müşteri kaybediyorsunuz DM'lerden?" ("How many clients do you lose through DMs?")
